Why education needs change

Teachers need time, and students need feedback

Teachers' time is under pressure

  • Marking, feedback and administration take many hours every week, often in the evenings and at weekends
  • Heavy workload is one of the main reasons teachers give for leaving the profession
  • When a teacher leaves, students lose experience, relationships and continuity

Source: Department for Education, Working Lives of Teachers and Leaders survey

Feedback drives deep learning

  • Timely, specific feedback is one of the most powerful influences on learning
  • Good feedback explains why, not only what, and this builds deep understanding
  • Late or general feedback arrives when students have already moved on

Source: Hattie & Timperley (2007), The Power of Feedback

The question for innovators: How can digital technology give teachers back their time, and give every student better feedback?
Lecture link AI as a General Purpose Technology: like electricity, it reshapes whole sectors, including education
Let's start with education itself. Teachers are the heart of every school. But today they are under huge pressure. Marking, feedback and paperwork take many hours, often at night and at weekends. In England, heavy workload is one of the main reasons teachers give for leaving the profession. When a good teacher leaves, students lose a lot. At the same time, research shows that good, timely feedback is one of the strongest ways to improve learning. So we have a real conflict: students need more feedback, but teachers have less time. [Click] In the lecture you learned that AI is a General Purpose Technology, like electricity. It will change every sector. I believe education is one of the sectors where it can make the biggest human difference.

Lecture link Opportunity recognition: great opportunities start from real problems
Here is a simple example. A laptop costs £360 after a 20% discount. What was the original price? This student multiplied by 1.2. [Click] It is wrong. Step 3 · Finding the gap

Real school work is handwritten, and that was the gap

Problem

→
Gap

Most digital tools mark multiple-choice or typed answers. But real school work is handwritten: multi-step maths, reasoning and diagrams. Teachers need marking that follows their mark scheme and gives credit for the method.

→
Opportunity

So where was the gap? Many digital tools can mark a multiple-choice quiz. That is the easy part. [Click] But real school work is handwritten. [Click] Nobody was solving this real, messy version of the problem in a way teachers could trust. That was our opportunity: automate the repetitive marking, give fast personalised feedback, and keep the teacher in charge. Step 4 · The innovation

How My Smart Teach works, with the teacher in control

1

Upload: the teacher uploads photos or scans of student work, plus the mark scheme

2

Read: AI reads the handwriting, including maths and diagrams

3

Check: a panel of AI assessors marks each answer against the same criteria, and the results are compared: “Reflective Consensus”, our patent-pending method

AI 1
AI 2
AI 3
→ consensus
4

Flag: anything unclear or uncertain is highlighted for the teacher

5

Approve: the teacher reviews, edits and approves, then personalised feedback goes to each student

AI proposes. Teachers decide.

Our review screen: the AI's reading, the mark and the feedback. The teacher can edit and approve.

Lecture link Human–AI collaboration · from the AI economy towards AI agents
I will explain the product in one slide, because today is about the journey. The teacher uploads the students' work and the mark scheme. AI reads the handwriting, including maths. Then comes our innovation. We do not rely on one AI answer. A panel of AI assessors marks the same answer, and we compare the results. It is like moderation in a school department: several markers, one mark scheme. We call it Reflective Consensus, and it is patent pending. Anything uncertain is highlighted for the teacher. The teacher approves, and every student receives personalised feedback. [Point to the screenshot] Here is our real review screen, with the same laptop question. You can see the AI's reading, the mark and the feedback, and the teacher can change anything. My short summary is: AI proposes, teachers decide.
